\section{Ethical Analysis}
Given the broadness of the issue presented in the essay, this section will be split into the core issues presented in section of the same name.

\subsection{Code Correctness}
As discussed in the technical section, AI utilizes the relationship between the provided prompt and the relationships it can build with the data to generate the code snippet. There is a major issue in this generation process, it doesn't perform logical reasoning in order to ensure that the code is correct. Tools like Claude utilize the added knowledge of the database, compiler errors and program output to ensure the program performs what is desired. However, this poses a critical issue, there is no guarantee that the side effects that stem from the lines being executed (such as memory allocation, pointer dereferencing, etc) don't harm the project or the users. This can be seen in several cases where the models are not trained with code of the quality and standards desired, which in turn leads to bugs \cite{ai-bugs-paper}.

According to an article by Computer World, Amazon recently fired 14000 employees because of AI being able to to perform their jobs at higher speeds instead of hiring more personnel \cite{amazon-ai-layoffs}. However, this presents ethical concerns under Kantianism. When companies use GenAI as a cost reduction method, they are using people as means to an end. First, the training data used to train the model was not obtained with explicit permission. Second, it disregards the customer's humanity by selling them a product that is more likely to have failures and not being transparent about the components made with GenAI.

In the context of act utilitarianism, the major costs involve the damages that a malformed product can cause and the benefits are the time and costs saved in business. When the software written with GenAI deploys, there are millions of people relying on it. A key example is the Amazon outage in early 2026, where the sellers and consumers get negatively impacted by the infrastructure crashing due to a GenAI induced bug and the engineers not catching it in review \cite{amazon-ai-guard-rails}. On the other hand, the saving of costs allowing companies like Google to generate more than half the code with GenAI \cite{google-ai-use} which allows the engineers and c-suite to make more money in the long term. When the infrastructure works and the cost is saved, only a select group of people benefit, however, this comes with the risk of harming millions in the process when a failure occurs.

From these two frameworks it can be seen that there are major issues that must be solved before GenAI can provide benefits to developers and companies that will outweigh the costs to the consumer. Given the irreversibility of the data trained, Kantianism will always weigh against GenAI used in production. However, if the benefits can outweigh the cost as the technology improves, it might become more ethically sound to rely on this technology.

\subsection{Damage to growth spaces for entry level engineers}
When discussing the usage of GenAI in software development, there is a core issue that can't be seen today, the lack of training for younger engineers. A remarkable example is the clothing simulation contact solver paper written for ZOZO and published in the ACM journal. This paper also has an implementation of the technique discussed in the paper on GitHub \cite{ppf-solver-whitepaper, ppf-solver}.

Under Kantianism, the development of the project is using people as means to an end as the code generated for the project was trained (the models being MS Copilot and Claude Code) with stolen data. In many universities and companies, there are entry level research positions, this is with the purpose of strengthening newer developers so there are more researchers in the future. With the usage of GenAI in a project like this, it removes the opportunity for a developer to acquire the skills in practice so that eventually they can innovate and research independently. This causes a contradiction as the researchers right now would not be where they are had they not been able to build such experience.

Under act utilitarianism, this issue arises in another context, the open source contribution space. In open source, most of the code written is by volunteers but used by industry with notable open source projects being Linux, Yocto, Chromium and Android. In the current job market, a way for entry level engineers to gather experience is to contribute to these projects. However, with the flood of GenAI contributions, lead contributors are being stretched thin with low quality code \cite{open-source-bother}. This is something that damages open source as it develops at a slower pace and in turn damages the whole industry. The cost of GenAI being used for code generation outweighs any of the benefits it has in the open source and commercial space when it comes to the opportunities for developers to grow.

Lastly, virtue ethics argues against using GenAI because an individual who chooses to go with GenAI is actively removing the chance of an entry developer to understand and cooperate with them in the future \cite{stack-overflow-junior-shortage}. This goes against the virtues of cooperation and sustainability.

\subsection{Displacement of people}
As discussed before, there are several costs to the usage of GenAI in software development, however, this is mainly discussed in the context of the industry. While focusing in the industry is required to be able to find the localized impacts, it is important to consider how the general public is also being disproportionately affected because of the infrastructure needed for this technology to exist \cite{people-displacement}.

This is not sound ethically under several frameworks. Under Kantianism, it is taking advantage of small communities in developing nations and damaging the environment of the region like the overexploited water resource in Mexico. Under virtue ethics, the individuals at companies that build and sell products using tools with massive negative environmental impacts are not practicing the virtues of collaboration, sustainability and impartiality. Lastly, under act utilitarianism, the usage of GenAI for commercial gain is sacrificing the long term gains of everyone given the carbon intensity of GenAI compute centers \cite{electricity-use}.
