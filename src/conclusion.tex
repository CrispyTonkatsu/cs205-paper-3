\section{Conclusion}
Based on the impacts GenAI has on the tech industry as well as outside, it can be seen that the sustainability of the decisions being made damages the ethical feasibility of the technology. If further developments reduce the damage caused to the general public and the environment it will be more ethically sound but the concerns where growth opportunities and work being stolen will not be fixed unless the training procedures being used by the GenAI engineers changes.

\section{Beyond the superficial}
The question of this essay is very relevant to the course as it is a very new technology with multiple facets to analyze. This is important because while now it is GenAI for code generation, in the future there will be new tools and procedures being used in the field. The tech industry is volatile due to it's wide range of applications, therefore it is important for engineers to be able to evaluate the ethics of the technologies being depended upon by the field.

This is very relevant to society at large as discussed in the ethical section, there are several issues that arise from this technology. The issues presented in this essay need to be more broadly discussed to encourage more positive adoptions of the technology to reduce the long term damage that is being currently done to the environment, the tech industry and the general public.

At a more personal level, this research has made me realize the wide impact this technology is having. It has made me think about how I apply the usage of GenAI. I mainly use it as a glorified documentation summarizer, but even then the research and understanding what it does makes my trust in it's correctness falter. I feel that there are ways to ensure there are guardrails that can be enforced such as code reviews, more statical analysis of the code with the tool and human made unit testing. However, at the end of the day, all of these practices are already best practices, so it really makes me wonder whether it is worth it to remove the space for someone else to grow, damage the environment and displace people I will never meet. If I need to learn this tool for survival I will do so \footnote{As an F-1 student, my stay in this country depends on me finding work in the field I am pursuing, and given this is an industry trend, specially among large companies, I don't have the luxury of selection.}, but I now know how the tool works, the limitations and that while there is no clear accountability chain, for me to practice the virtues of honesty and fairness I should strive to be a person who knows the origins of my privileges and use them for bettering the world. While this is easy to say it is hard to practice, this field as mentioned before, is very volatile, so to be a good practitioner in the field, I need to understand not only how things work but how they affect those around me.
